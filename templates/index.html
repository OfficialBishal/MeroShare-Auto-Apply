The 5-digit code in parentheses next to your broker on the MeroShare login dropdown. Example: "NIMB ACE CAPITAL LIMITED (10600)" → enter 10600.
The number you type into MeroShare's Username field on the login screen — typically the last 6–7 digits of your full BOID. For example, a BOID of 1301010012345678 would have a username of 12345678.
Your MeroShare login password. Stored in the OS keychain (encrypted by your login password), never in plain files.
The 4-digit PIN you enter on MeroShare's apply form (the last step before submission). Different from your login password.
Customer Reference Number from your bank. Visible on MeroShare's apply form when you start a new application — typically starts with letters then digits ("CB1234567"). Each bank account has its own CRN.
